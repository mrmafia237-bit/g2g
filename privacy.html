G2G DIS BOT - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

G2G DIS BOT provides Discord server administration tools, including role management, department-based permissions, activity logging, and a management dashboard.

2. Information We Process

Depending on enabled features, the bot may process Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, server IDs, role IDs, channel IDs, member join and leave events, role changes, message edits and deletions (including available message content), voice channel activity, moderation events, and dashboard authentication data.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to operate the bot, enforce role-management permissions, maintain administrative settings, and provide activity and audit logs.

4. Storage and Access

The application stores configuration and role-management audit information. Activity logs may be posted to Discord channels selected by server administrators. Administrative access is restricted through Discord authentication and configured permissions.

5. Third-Party Services

The bot uses Discord's API and authentication services. Discord's own policies also apply to data handled by its platform.

6. Retention and Deletion

Retention depends on the application's storage configuration and server logging settings. Requests to review or delete stored information may be directed to the G2G DIS BOT administrator.

7. Security

Access controls are used to protect administrative functions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Policy Updates

Updates to this policy will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the G2G DIS BOT administrator through the official G2G CITY Discord community.
