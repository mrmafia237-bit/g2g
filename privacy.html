G2G Discord Bots - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the G2G Discord applications operated by the G2G team: G2G DIS BOT, G2G LOGS, G2G TXADMIN, and G2G Subscriptions. Each application processes information only as needed for its enabled features.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the application and enabled features, we may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, usernames and display names, role and membership information, commands and interactions, moderation actions, and audit events. Logging features may process member joins and departures, role changes, message edits or deletions and available message content, and voice-channel events. Subscription features may process subscription status, expiry dates, and associated Discord IDs. Administrative access features may process eligibility and authorization records. Dashboard features may process Discord authentication information.

Not every application uses every category listed above. Access to Discord data depends on the application's actual configuration and permissions.

3. Purposes

Information is used to operate commands, enforce authorized access, manage roles and subscriptions, record administrative and server activity, troubleshoot problems, and protect service integrity.

4. Storage and Access

Configuration, authorization records, subscription records, and audit information may be stored by the relevant application. Logs may be delivered to Discord channels configured by server administrators. Access is limited through configured permissions and administrative controls. Storage practices may vary by application and deployment.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

These applications use Discord's platform and APIs. Information may be visible to authorized administrators in designated logging channels or dashboards. Discord's own terms and privacy policy apply to its services. We do not sell Discord user data.

6. Retention and Requests

Information is retained as needed for the relevant application's operational, security, and administrative purposes, subject to its configuration and applicable requirements. For questions about access, correction, or deletion of data controlled by G2G, contact the G2G application administrators through the official G2G CITY Discord community. Some information stored directly on Discord may need to be managed through Discord or the relevant server administrators.

7. Security

We use access restrictions and administrative controls to reduce unauthorized access. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the G2G application administrators through the official G2G CITY Discord community.
